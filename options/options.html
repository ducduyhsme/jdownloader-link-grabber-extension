💡 How It Works

  • Decrypters (Intermediate Shortlinks): Automatically detects multi-step decryption links and forwards them to JDownloader 2 LinkGrabber to crawl the final downloadable file.
  • File-Hosters (Cloud & File Sharing): Captures links from file hosting providers (Mega.nz, Fshare.vn, Rapidgator, 1fichier, Google Drive, YouTube, etc.).
  • Easy Addition: When visiting an unlisted website, simply right-click or open the extension menu and click "Add to Database".
